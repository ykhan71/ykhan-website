Reflection

A Room Without Clocks

There is a kind of quiet that does not ask anything of you — the way a window holds the light without deciding what it means. I have been learning to sit in that. To let the afternoon go grey without chasing it back to gold. Some rooms are built for thinking. Others, for the slow work of forgetting how to count.

Jun 2026

Observation

Signal

Everything speaks if you stay still long enough — the market, the marriage, the body before it breaks. We mistake silence for absence. But silence is just the gap between what is said and what is meant. I have been listening to the things that do not move. They are the loudest.
