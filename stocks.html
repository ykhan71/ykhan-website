Aug 2026

Semiconductor cycle: where are we?

AI infrastructure spend appears durable but the memory cycle recovery is the key variable to watch heading into H2. LRCX and the equipment names are a leading indicator — order trends will tell us before results do.

Jul 2026

On NVDA's moat

CUDA's lock-in is real but shouldn't be taken for granted. AMD's ROCm improvements and custom ASIC proliferation (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium, Meta MTIA) are worth watching. Not a bear thesis — but something to track.
